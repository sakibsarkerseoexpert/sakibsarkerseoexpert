## AI search: AEO and GEO

People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI tools for recommendations, so I've made **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** part of every engagement. The goal is the same as classic SEO: be the brand that gets found and chosen, whether the question is typed into Google or asked to an AI assistant.

## How I work

| | Step | What it means |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| **01** | **Site structure** | A clean, crawl-friendly foundation comes first. |
| **02** | **Search intent** | Content mapped to what people really look for. |
| **03** | **Entity & schema** | Credibility that search engines and AI trust. |
| **04** | **Earned links** | Authority built through relevance, not volume. |

**Engagement model.** Most of my work is long-term retainers. I stay on to track algorithm updates, refresh underperforming pages, and adjust the strategy as search changes.

**Clean-up first.** Many clients come to me after being burned by keyword-stuffing "SEO experts". I clean up thin content and toxic backlinks first, then build sustainably on top.

> **I work directly with every client.** No account managers, no outsourced writers, no black-box reporting. Every deliverable is something I did myself and can explain line by line.
