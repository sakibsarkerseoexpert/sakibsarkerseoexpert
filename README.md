### AI Optimization (AIO) — five pillars

1. **AI-friendly content** — clear answers, in-depth coverage, structured writing
2. **E-E-A-T** — real expertise, author information, reliable sources, updated content
3. **Structured data** — FAQ, Article, Organization, Product & Review schema
4. **Technical SEO** — fast, mobile-friendly, clean HTML, HTTPS, internal linking
5. **Authority** — quality backlinks, trusted mentions, original research and case studies

> **Formula:** Technical SEO + High-Quality Content + E-E-A-T + Structured Data + Authority = AI Optimization success
