<div align="center">
<img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&weight=700&size=26&duration=2200&pause=1000&color=7C3AED&center=true&vCenter=true&repeat=false&width=500&height=46&lines=%F0%9F%92%BC+About+Me" alt="About Me"/>
</div>

I'm an SEO expert based in Bangladesh. For more than **seven years** I've helped e-commerce brands, SaaS companies, agencies, local businesses, and creators get found by the people who are actually searching for them. So far that adds up to **2,000+ SEO and content projects**.

My approach starts with **content and search intent**, then builds the technical, structural, and off-page work on top so the content can rank and keep ranking. More recently I've made **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** part of every engagement, because people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI tools for recommendations, and businesses need to show up there too.

Most of my work is **long-term retainers**. I stay on to track algorithm updates, refresh underperforming pages, and adjust the strategy as search changes. Many clients come to me after being burned by keyword-stuffing "SEO experts", so I clean up thin content and toxic backlinks first, then build sustainably on top.

> ### 💬 I work directly with every client.
> No account managers, no outsourced writers, no black-box reporting. Every deliverable is something I did myself and can explain line by line.
