## 👋 About Me

I'm **Sakib Sarker**, an SEO specialist based in Bangladesh. I help businesses improve their organic search visibility, attract relevant visitors, and build a stronger presence across traditional search engines and emerging AI-powered discovery platforms.

With **7+ years of experience and 2,000+ projects completed**, my work spans technical SEO, on-page optimization, content strategy, local SEO, e-commerce SEO, and authority building. I also work on **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** to help brands become easier for AI-powered search and answer tools to understand and reference.

My approach is practical: understand the business, research the audience and competitors, identify the biggest opportunities, prioritize fixes, and measure progress. I focus on sustainable improvements—not empty promises of instant rankings.

> **My working principle:** Clear strategy, careful execution, transparent communication, and reporting clients can understand.

## 🧭 My SEO Process

| Step | Focus | What happens |
|:---:|---|---|
| **01** | 🔍 Discovery & Audit | Understand goals, audience, website health, and current search performance. |
| **02** | 🎯 Keyword & Intent Research | Map relevant queries to the right pages and user needs. |
| **03** | 🏗️ Technical Foundation | Address crawlability, indexation, architecture, speed, and structured data issues. |
| **04** | ✍️ Content Optimization | Improve usefulness, relevance, page structure, and internal links. |
| **05** | 🌐 Authority & Brand Signals | Identify relevant outreach, citation, and brand-mention opportunities. |
| **06** | 📈 Measure & Improve | Review available data, report progress, and refine the strategy over time. |
